## R1 ? Make It Useful

- **R1.0 Foundation Alignment (this authorized task):** preserve/archive baseline;
  align local/manual contracts, safe config, additive lineage and synthetic tests.
- **R1.1 (not authorized):** first permitted real signal through local launcher,
  Python API, single durable worker and minimal usable browser screen. G01-G03.
- R1.2: basic bilingual dedupe/clustering, immutable event revisions and temporal
  NEW/SEEN/UPDATE behavior; timezone-aware view rollover.
- R1.3: evidence-linked intelligence, optional angles, free-only provider checks,
  separate scoring/cache and usage reservation. G04 before AI activation.
- R1.4: full confirmed sections, structured decisions/settings, article URL link,
  accurate impressions and progressive results; no writing editor.
- R1.5: repeated use/recovery, rights-aware retention, backup/restore, baseline and
  real pilot review. G05/G06. No three-session claim based on synthetic fixtures.

Stop after every authorized slice with tested handoff, commit and reviewable branch.
R1.0 passing tests does not satisfy all R1-D01-D24 release acceptance requirements.

## Later product releases

- R2 Make It Smart: user-requested story/angle/question research, rich evidence,
  coverage and supported social extensions. No automatic costly agent per story.
- R3 Make It Learn: authorized performance data/imports and native metric linkage;
  Marfeel remains an independent optional connector.
- R4 Make It Better: evaluated candidate calibration, owner approval and rollback;
  no automatic weight updates or causal traffic claims.
- R5 Make It a Product: adoption-gated organization isolation, CMS/hosting,
  business validation, optional automation. Details remain owner decisions.

Three-month direction is better discovery, stronger research and outcome linkage,
not an invented deadline or proof of prediction quality. Keep deferred ideas in
PRODUCT_BACKLOG; no cloud, scheduling, paid services or R1.1 work in this task.
